Record Portal Payment

v1
For payments you made on a vendor's own website — Sysco, PFG, Pepsi. Pick the vendor, enter what you paid, tick the invoices it covered, and attach the screenshot of the payment. The bills come off Accounts Payable and the cost stays exactly where it was.

1 Which vendor did you pay?

Only vendors set up as "we get a bill and pay it later" appear here.

2 What did you pay?

Copy these off the payment confirmation

3 Which invoices did it cover?

Pick a vendor first.

4 Credits on this payment — only if there were any

A credit shows on the remittance as a negative line. There is no bill behind it, so record it here — it counts toward the total but nothing is marked paid.

5 Invoices not in the system — only if the remittance shows one that isn't listed above

Adding one here creates the food cost as well as the bill, so it lands on the P&L straight away. That is the point — it is money you paid that was never logged. But the venue has to be right, because nothing downstream can correct it for you.

6 Attach the screenshot

iPhone screenshots are fine. A photo taken on an iPhone may be HEIC, which will be rejected — take a screenshot instead, or set Settings → Camera → Formats → Most Compatible.
